# B, Le relief: references

Thumbnails are kept for study only; each source and licence is listed.

### `carte-arch-ologique-et-topographique-des-ruines-de-carthage.jpg`
[Carte archéologique et topographique des Ruines de Carthage. Echelle 1 - 5.000 - Bordy - btv1b105803532 (3 of 3).jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Carte_arch%C3%A9ologique_et_topographique_des_Ruines_de_Carthage._Echelle_1_-_5.000_-_Bordy_-_btv1b105803532_(3_of_3).jpg), Public domain

**Borrowed:** Carthage 1895: the title set in widely spaced engraved capitals, a dotted coastline, hairline graticule ticks in the margin. Borrowed: dotted shoreline and margin ticks.

### `schulkarte-der-schweiz-prof-ed-imhof-btv1b531983954-jpg.jpg`
[Schulkarte der Schweiz, Prof. Ed. Imhof - btv1b531983954.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Schulkarte_der_Schweiz,_Prof._Ed._Imhof_-_btv1b531983954.jpg), Public domain

**Borrowed:** Imhof's school map: lit slopes warm, shadows cool blue-grey (aerial perspective). Borrowed: shadow tone #8E9AA8 in hillshade, never grey.

### `swisstopo-000-410-909-neue-landeskarte-erstellung-relief-tif.jpg`
[Swisstopo-000-410-909-Neue Landeskarte-Erstellung Relief.tif](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Swisstopo-000-410-909-Neue_Landeskarte-Erstellung_Relief.tif), Attribution-Swisstopo

**Borrowed:** A swisstopo cartographer painting the relief by hand on a tilted desk. Borrowed: the north-west light at 315° and the idea of the map as a made object.

### `usgs-topographic-map-michigan-wyandotte-quadrangle-278613-19.jpg`
[USGS topographic map, Michigan, Wyandotte quadrangle (278613), 1906, 1-62500.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:USGS_topographic_map,_Michigan,_Wyandotte_quadrangle_(278613),_1906,_1-62500.jpg), Public domain

**Borrowed:** USGS quad: brown index contours with their value set in the line, blue water lining along the shore. Borrowed: water lining (3 parallel lines along the coast) and labels set inside contour gaps.
