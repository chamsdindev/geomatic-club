# D, Les calques: self-critique

Frames: `../_out/d-calques/` (r0 = first build, r1 = after round 1, r2 = after round 2).

## Round 1 (r0 → r1)
1. The exploded stack ran off the left edge and the plates crowded each other: plates further apart (64 px),
   a firmer film edge, so each plate reads as its own sheet.
2. « Golfe de Tunis » ran off its plate: moved inside, on the gulf's axis.
3. The member's label had no halo: white halo (paint-order stroke), as cartographers letter names over detail.
4. « 14 h 30 » broke across lines: non-breaking spaces in every time.
5. The empty stack started too washed out: unregistered plates at 50 % instead of 38 %.

## Round 2 (r1 → r2)
1. The stack still overlapped the header: smaller (0.72), lower, moved right, clipped to its stage.
2. The main event was too subtle (9 px off register): unregistered plates now sit 16 px off **and turned by about
   2°**, like a misregistered proof; when a field becomes valid its plate snaps into register and its registration
   marks flash red, as on a press proof.

Still open: the light-table grey under the flattened map before the attribute table rises (0.6 s of empty area);
the plates' tabs are small at this angle.
