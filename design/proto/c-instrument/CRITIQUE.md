## Round 1 (r0 → r1)
1. FIX collided with the fix ladder: moved into the state row, its own slot.
2. The fiche ran off the bottom: the sky plot folds away before printing, the fiche scrolls if needed.
3. « Rendez-vous » and the countdown wrapped: label above, value large below.
4. Decimal commas looked spaced out in B612 Mono (« 6, 8 »): the LCD uses a decimal point, like survey
   controllers; the fiche keeps the French comma in the proportional face.
5. Satellite labels G05 and R10 overlapped: R10 moved to its own azimuth.
6. The long French date wrapped inside the well: the well shows « sam. 14 nov. 2026 », the fiche the full date.

## Round 2 (r1 → r2)
1. The climax was too quiet (thin dot-matrix FIX, 46 px): the whole LCD now inverts at the lock, FIX at 76 px.
   Instruments flash on a fix; this is that moment.
2. The fiche's coordinates still used the monospace: now proportional with tabular figures, « 36,8245° N ».
3. The countdown bar was decorative stripes: it's now a levelling staff, one red or white segment per day left,
   numbered every 10 days (ref: Nivellierlatte).
4. The bubble overlapped the vial's centre ring: smaller bubble, dashed target ring, as on a circular level.
5. The field book had no epoch: an « Époque » row (date and time of the measurement).
