# Geomatic Club — design (redesign, branch `redesign`)

Status, 27 October 2026: round 1 (`design/CONCEPTS.md`) rejected; round 2 (`design/CONCEPTS-2.md`: references, three
GSAP prototypes, logo exploration) waiting for Chams's choice. This file
gets the full design (tokens, type scale, layout, GSAP timelines, sound map, logo usage) once a direction is chosen.

## What the first version got wrong (never again)

1. The background was wallpaper: pale contour lines, the most predictable "geography" image, low contrast
   everywhere, no focal point.
2. Geomatics lived behind the form, not in it: a standard underlined form on a backdrop; typing did nothing a
   surveyor would recognise.
3. Timid colour: grey-green, navy, thin sage, and the one accent absent from the main screen.
4. Type without a voice: a wide bold wordmark plus plain text, every size alike; native date fields in English.
5. A logo nobody could own (a triangle with a dot reads as a warning sign or a play button), and an intro that
   made people wait 3.7 s through sci-fi HUD clichés before they could act.

## Logo brief

- **Club**: Geomatic Club, a student club of geomatics (surveying, GNSS, remote sensing, GIS, cartography) in Tunisia.
- **Audience**: geomatics and geography students in Tunisia, 18–25; their teachers; the firms that hire them.
  They know a theodolite, a sky plot and a Landsat band by sight.
- **Personality**: precise, curious, field-going, proud of the craft; a club, not a company: warm, never corporate.
- **Where it lives**: T-shirts and caps (embroidery, one colour), Instagram avatar (circle), posters, a rubber
  stamp, stickers on survey gear, this site (favicon, header, share image), slides.
- **Must**: work at 16 px and on a T-shirt back, in one colour and reversed; come from the world of geomatics;
  be drawable by hand on a grid.
- **Must not**: globe, generic map pin, pin + wifi waves, gradients, the old triangle.
- **Directions**: round 1 (`design/logo/`) rejected as too close to existing symbols; round 2 (`design/logo2/`):
  twenty thumbnails, three refined (Vectorisation, Cheminement, Hachures), Cheminement recommended.
